About This Object Chawan (Tea Bowl)

The chawan, or tea bowl, is one of the most important objects used in the Japanese tea ceremony. Matcha is prepared and served inside the bowl, making it both a functional and artistic object within tea traditions.

Tea bowls were carefully crafted by ceramic artists and often reflected ideas of simplicity, balance, and harmony connected to chanoyu, the Japanese tea ceremony. Different shapes, textures, and glazes were used depending on the season and style of tea gathering, allowing each bowl to create a unique experience for the participant.
