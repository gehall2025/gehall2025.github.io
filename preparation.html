About This Object Chasen (Bamboo Whisk)

The chasen, or bamboo whisk, is used to mix matcha powder with hot water during tea preparation. Its fine bamboo prongs help create the smooth texture and foam associated with traditional matcha tea.

Crafted from a single piece of bamboo, the whisk demonstrates the precision and craftsmanship valued within Japanese tea culture. The chasen became an essential tool in tea ceremonies because it allowed tea masters to properly prepare matcha while emphasizing focus, care, and ritual within the experience.
