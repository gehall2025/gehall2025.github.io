About This Object Chashaku (Tea Scoop)

The chashaku, or tea scoop, is a small utensil used to transfer matcha powder into the tea bowl before preparation. Traditionally made from bamboo, the scoop reflects the simplicity and elegance associated with Japanese tea ceremonies.

Tea masters carefully measured matcha using the chashaku to ensure balance and consistency during preparation. Although small in size, the object plays an important role in the ceremonial process and highlights the attention to detail and mindfulness present within chanoyu traditions.
